# Demo Video Placeholder & Instructions

Drop your screen/webcam recording of the live gesture recognition demo in this directory.

### Recommended Format & Details
- **Filename**: `gesture_demo.mp4` (or `gesture_demo.webm` / `gesture_demo.gif`)
- **Resolution**: 720p or 1080p at 30 FPS
- **Length**: ~20–60 seconds
- **Demonstration Checklist**:
  1. Show your hand performing all 4 classes in sequence:
     - `open_palm`
     - `fist`
     - `peace`
     - `thumbs_up`
  2. Move your hand closer and further from the camera to show scale invariance.
  3. Rotate and tilt your hand slightly to show rotation invariance.
  4. Notice the on-screen bounding box, predicted class badge, confidence score, and real-time FPS overlay generated by `deploy.py`.

> [!NOTE]
> Video binaries are gitignored via `.gitignore` (`demo/*.mp4`, `demo/*.mov`, etc.) to prevent bloating git repositories. If submitting as a zip/archive or course portal, place the file directly here.
